A commercial HVAC manufacturer wanted something that did not exist yet: cloud apps to control their air-curtain units remotely, across multiple sites, from both web and mobile. The challenge was making industrial hardware feel as simple as a thermostat.

## Grounding it in research

Before any screens, I ran surveys, interviews, and a competitive scan to find where operators actually struggle. The findings became a technology map, a shared blueprint that kept engineers and design aligned on what was realistic to build.

## Designing the flows

Three flows carried the product. Onboarding had to make account setup feel like a welcome, not a hurdle. Unit pairing had to walk someone through connecting real hardware step by step. And the general flow had to make moving through the app obvious. Each one was built to take the guesswork out.

## From sketch to screen

Early concepts and wireframes set the structure first, navigation and information architecture, before any visual polish. That kept the dense controls of industrial HVAC legible instead of intimidating.

<Figure
  src={concepts}

## Outcome

After launch the bounce rate dropped noticeably. Operators called out the streamlined navigation, the easier scanning, faster access to the units they needed, and a finish that finally matched the hardware it controlled.
