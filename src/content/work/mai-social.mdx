MAI Social set out to do social networking differently: a personalised feed, real tools for political engagement, and communities built around shared interests, with AI running underneath. I led the design of its core.

## What we were building

The goals were specific. A feed people could shape with their own filters. A politics space that surfaced genuinely relevant content. And Circles, a way to find and connect with people who care about the same things you do.

## My role

I was lead designer across the feed, profile, Circles, notifications, messaging, and the MAI AI chat, from first concept through prototyping and refinement. The feed leaned on small, deliberate motion to make browsing and filtering feel alive rather than mechanical. Profiles, notifications, and threaded messages were built for clarity over decoration.

## Circles

Circles connect people, so the design made that literal. A built-in map view lets members see each other geographically, which turns an abstract interest group into something closer to a real community.

## AI and politics

The MAI AI chat takes both text and voice, so it meets people however they prefer to communicate. MAI Politics went further: a dynamic quiz that captures a person's views, interactive maps, official profiles, AI-assisted browsing, and built-in fundraising tools.

## Outcome

The platform secured investor funding and drove real engagement, carried by the personalised feed and the political tools. Through all of it the design held one line: powerful features, but always centred on the person using them.
